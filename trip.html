A FLEXIBLE FIRST DRAFT

Five days, at your pace.

01
ARRIVE & SETTLE

Guwahati to Shillong

Arrive, take the road to Shillong, and keep the evening free. A slow landing helps the rest of the trip feel less rushed.

Transfer dayEasy eveningRoad time to verify
A gentler option

Stay near your arrival point if the flight comes in late. Confirm transfer time and conditions close to travel.

02
SHILLONG, SLOWLY

A little city, then an open afternoon

Pick one neighbourhood or viewpoint, then leave the rest of the day loose for a café, a market, or a much-needed nap.

Choose one stopFood & local life
Choose your energy

Low-key: keep the afternoon near your stay. More active: add a short local walk after checking the weather.

03
THE ROAD TO SOHRA

Take the scenic route south

Pause at one or two viewpoints on the way. Keep the stops flexible rather than turning the drive into a checklist.

Scenic transferRain can change the plan
Worth checking before you go

Road and weather conditions can change. Leave buffer time and confirm current local advice before setting out.

04
YOUR KIND OF OUTDOORS

Choose a walk—or choose not to

Make this the flexible day: an easy viewpoint option or a longer guided walk, depending on conditions and how you feel.

Optional activityEffort variesLocal guide may help
Accessibility note

Some popular trails have many uneven steps. Check current route details and match the walk to everyone in your group.

RETURN WITH A BUFFER

Sohra back to Guwahati

Keep the final transfer unhurried and allow a sensible buffer before your onward journey.

Transfer dayCheck departure time
