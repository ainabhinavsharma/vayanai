# Ladakh High Altitude Acclimatization and Health Protocol
[DOC:ladakh_altitude_protocol.txt]
1. Mandatory Rest: Non-negotiable medical advice for all arrivals into Leh (elevation 3,500 meters) by air is mandatory resting for the first 36 to 48 hours. No strenuous excursions or elevation gains during this window.
2. High Altitude Pulmonary Edema (HAPE) Prevention: Travelers must drink 3 to 4 liters of water daily. Avoid alcohol, smoking, and heavy exertion during the initial 72 hours.
3. Vulnerable Groups: For elderly travelers and young children, an oxygen saturation pulse oximeter check is mandatory twice daily. Keep hospital emergency transport coordinates on hand.
4. Pass Crossings: Khardung La (5,359m) and Chang La (5,360m) visits must not exceed 20 to 30 minutes of stay at the pass summit to avoid acute mountain sickness (AMS).
5. Permits: Protected Area Permits (PAP) and Inner Line Permits (ILP) are mandatory for Nubra Valley, Pangong Tso, and Tso Moriri. Carry multiple physical copies.
