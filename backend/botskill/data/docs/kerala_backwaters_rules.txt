# Kerala Backwaters & Coastal Tourism Regulations
[DOC:kerala_backwaters_rules.txt]
1. Night Cruising Prohibition: In Vembanad Lake and Alappuzha canals, houseboats are strictly prohibited from moving between 6:00 PM and 6:00 AM to safeguard local fishing operations.
2. Eco-Friendly Operations: Houseboats must possess Green Palm certification with functional bio-toilets and zero plastic discharge into waterways.
3. Cultural Etiquette: When visiting coastal villages or traditional courtyard dining locations, modest attire covering shoulders and knees is appreciated.
4. Food Walks: Morning fish auction walks in Fort Kochi must be conducted with licensed local heritage storytellers.
