# Meghalaya Travel Safety, Trails, and Permits
[DOC:meghalaya_safety_and_permits.txt]
1. Weather and Monsoons: The Cherrapunji and Sohra plateau experiences dense fog and torrential rainfall between May and September. High-volume waterfalls require mandatory barriers.
2. Living Root Bridges: The double-decker living root bridge in Nongriat involves approximately 3,500 steep stone steps each way. For travellers with limited mobility or elders, step-free vehicular viewpoints such as Nohkalikai falls viewpoint and Mawsmai Eco Park are recommended.
3. Cave Exploration: Mawsmai cave is well-lit and accessible for general visitors. Arwah cave features limestone fossils and requires flat walking shoes; inner submerged cave systems require authorized local Khasi guides.
4. Local Community Respect: Sacred Groves in Mawphlang prohibit the removal of any leaf, pebble, or branch. Travelers must be accompanied by an accredited local custodian.
5. Pacing and Transfers: Road travel between Guwahati and Shillong takes 3 to 4 hours. Mountain roads to Sohra require daytime driving; night transfers after 6:00 PM are discouraged due to thick fog.
