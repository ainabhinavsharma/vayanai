# Sohra Indigenous Stays Collective — Partner Terms & Facilities
[DOC:sohra_homestay_partner_terms.txt]
1. Property Description: Traditional stone-clad eco-cottages perched on the southern rim of the Sohra ridge overlooking the Bangladesh plains.
2. Accessibility: Two ground-floor deluxe cottages feature ramp access, step-free wide doorways, walk-in showers with grab rails, and parking within 10 meters of the cottage door.
3. Cancellation & Rescheduling: Free cancellation is permitted up to 72 hours prior to scheduled check-in. Rescheduling due to extreme monsoon landslides is accommodated without fee.
4. Meals: Farm-to-table Khasi and North Indian meals prepared with vegetables from the community cooperative garden. Pure vegetarian and vegan dietary options available with 24 hours advance notice.
5. Inquiries: All booking inquiries must specify dates, party size, and mobility requirements. Staged partner quotes remain valid for 48 hours from issuance.
